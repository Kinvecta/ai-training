# Syllabus

Kinvecta AI Training is a free course on using generative AI well and safely at work: eight core sessions and two technical deep dives, about 1 hour 42 minutes of video in English (about 2 hours in Spanish). Each video is also a 90-minute classroom module (120 minutes for the deep dives), so trainers can run it with their own teams.

Watch in order from Session 1. The series follows five stages of AI use: **1** safe everyday use, **2** the API in a container, **3** fixed workflows, **4** tool calling, **5** agents and skills. Everyone starts at Stage 1.

Titles below are the full video titles. Session folders use a short form of each. If you have limited time, or a specific role, the [learning paths](../docs/learning-paths.md) suggest what to watch and in what order; the [glossary](../docs/glossary.md) defines every term the course uses and points to the session that teaches it.

## By the end you can

- Explain what an LLM is, what a harness is, and the difference between a chatbot, a workflow and an agent.
- Keep your organization able to switch models, vendors and tools without rebuilding.
- Put the right data in the right tool, prompt with context, and verify every claim before it is used.
- Reason about tokens, context windows, caching and the ways AI tools find your data.
- Call a model from your own code safely: endpoint in config, pinned versions, a container with one way out.
- Turn a repeat task into a fixed workflow with checks and human approval.
- Connect tools over MCP and defend against prompt injection.
- Package know-how as reviewed skills, put guardrails on agents, and measure whether training worked.
- (Deep dives) Build and test an MCP server on the current spec, and ship containers through a scanned, signed CI/CD pipeline.

The English playlist is [Kinvecta AI Training: Using Generative AI Well and Safely at Work](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLQqgikRVyDAM). La versión en español está en [`es/`](../es/) y en [su propia lista de YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLPYZqM8a_06M).
